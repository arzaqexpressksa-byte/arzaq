# Arzaq Elite Web

Design and develop a world-class premium corporate website for Arzaq Express Industrial Est. This is an industrial engineering company serving the Middle East (Saudi Arabia/Dubai region), so the design language must communicate trust, engineering excellence, luxury, precision, professionalism, and enterprise quality.

The content, services, business information, headings, mission, vision, values, contact information, and every business detail must be taken exactly from the provided PDF. Do not rewrite or summarize unless necessary for better readability. Preserve the business identity completely.
